# Activity Tracker — store listing draft

## App name
Activity Tracker

## Short description
Track workouts, meals, habits and goals, with cross-device sync and PDF plans.

## Full description
Build a clearer picture of your daily fitness routine with Activity Tracker by Viral Systems.

• Log workouts and training time.
• Record meals, calories and macronutrients.
• Track habits by date and follow your completion history.
• Organize daily tasks and measurable goals.
• Save body measurements and weight history.
• Review a weekly workout template and estimated nutrition targets.
• Ask the built-in Fitness Coach about supported fitness topics.
• Save workout and nutrition plans as PDFs.
• Sign in with Google to access the same records on the website and Android app.
• Try guest access and link Google in Profile to keep records across devices.
• Delete your account and app records from Profile.

The coach uses built-in rules and templates. It is not a live AI, certified trainer or medical device. The app does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent medical conditions. Fitness plans and nutrition estimates are general information; seek a qualified healthcare professional's advice for your health and circumstances. Intended for adults aged 18 and over.

Internet access is required for sign-in and synchronization. Guest access alone does not recover records on another device. No advertisements or paid AI API are included.
